How to play

  • Fill in the 5 missing middle words of the chain.
  • Every neighboring pair makes a phrase or compound word, like SILVER → LINING → FABRIC.
  • Tap any open row to jump to it, or use the up and down arrow keys. Solve in any order.
  • A wrong guess keeps the letters in the right spot and briefly shows yellow (wrong spot) or gray (not in the word).
  • Your score drops for hints, wrong guesses and slow solves.
  • Finish a chain and start another. There is no daily limit.
