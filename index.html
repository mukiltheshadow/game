How to play

  • Fill in the 5 missing middle words of the chain.
  • Every neighboring pair makes a phrase or compound word, like SILVER → LINING → FABRIC.
  • Tap any open row to jump to it and solve in any order.
  • A wrong guess keeps the letters that are in the right spot and turns the rest yellow (wrong spot) or gray (not in the word) briefly.
  • Score starts at 100. Hints, wrong guesses and slow solves cost points.
  • Finish a chain and start another. There is no daily limit.
